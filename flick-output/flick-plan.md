# Flick Plan

## Transcript
Written on-screen script drawn from the NovaMind AI landing page (`index.html`). There is no voiceover.

- 0.0–4.5s: Your team is drowning in busywork.
- 4.5–8.0s: Meet NovaMind AI.
- 8.0–14.0s: Custom AI agents that run your workflows end to end.
- 14.0–19.0s: 10x faster workflows. 85% lower costs. Running 24/7.
- 19.0–24.0s: Live in days, not months.
- 24.0–30.0s: Join the waitlist — 500+ already have.

## Format
9:16 vertical, 1080×1920 at 30 fps, 30 seconds total.

## Brand assets
No files were supplied. The look follows the landing page's own design: background #06060a, surface #0e0e14, border #1a1a2e, gradient #7c3aed → #2563eb → #06b6d4, accent #a78bfa, and Inter type.

## Creative direction
Defaults chosen by Claude because no direction was given: a sleek, dark, premium SaaS feel that matches the landing page. Each line gets a concrete product visual, such as task cards, a workflow graph, counters, or a waitlist form. Motion is snappy and spring-based. There is no background music.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — busywork-overload — 0.0–4.5s
Transcript: "Your team is drowning in busywork."
What's on screen: Task cards pile up in a tilted, messy stack in the center: "Invoice #4821", "Follow up with lead", "Weekly report", "Update CRM", "Schedule call", "Reply to support ticket". A red "Hours lost this week" counter climbs at the top.
Text on screen: "Your team is drowning in busywork."
Sequential / interaction: The cards drop in one by one, each slightly rotated. The counter climbs from 0 to 23h. The headline slams in at the end.
Sound effect: Popups.mp3 as the cards start dropping; Impact.mp3 when the headline lands.
Audio-coupled idea: The first card drop syncs with the pop; the headline's scale-in syncs with the impact.
Transition: The cards are pulled toward the center (they continue into Scene 2).

### Scene 2 — meet-novamind — 4.5–8.0s
Transcript: "Meet NovaMind AI."
What's on screen: The leftover task cards spiral into a glowing purple orb. The orb bursts into the gradient "NovaMind AI" wordmark, with the green-dot badge "Now accepting early access".
Text on screen: "Meet", "NovaMind AI", "Now accepting early access"
Sequential / interaction: Cards are absorbed, the orb pulses, the wordmark reveals, then the badge pops in.
Sound effect: riser.mp3 during absorption; aha-moment.MP3 at the reveal.
Transition: Crossfade.

### Scene 3 — agent-runs-workflow — 8.0–14.0s
Transcript: "Custom AI agents that run your workflows end to end."
What's on screen: A vertical workflow of four node cards (Lead captured → Personalized email sent → CRM updated → Invoice paid), linked by a glowing connector. An "AI Agent" pulse travels down it. Each node gets a green check as the pulse reaches it.
Text on screen: "Custom AI agents" / "that run your workflows end to end." plus the node labels.
Sequential / interaction: The nodes appear, then the pulse travels and each node flips to done.
Sound effect: Correct.mp3 on each check (4×).
Transition: Slide up.

### Scene 4 — results-count-up — 14.0–19.0s
Transcript: "10x faster workflows. 85% lower costs. Running 24/7."
What's on screen: Three big stat cards stacked vertically, with numbers counting up in the gradient.
Text on screen: "10x" Faster workflows, "85%" Lower costs, "24/7" Always running.
Sequential / interaction: The cards slide in one by one and each number counts up.
Sound effect: Pop.mp3 as each card enters.
Transition: Zoom.

### Scene 5 — live-in-days — 19.0–24.0s
Transcript: "Live in days, not months."
What's on screen: A calendar strip where "months" gets struck through, then three numbered steps tick by: Tell us your pain points → We build your agent → Launch & optimize. A progress bar fills.
Text on screen: "Live in days," "~~not months~~", plus the three step labels.
Sequential / interaction: The strike-through animates, the steps light up in sequence, and the bar fills.
Sound effect: Click.mp3 on each step.
Transition: Crossfade.

### Scene 6 — join-the-waitlist — 24.0–30.0s
Transcript: "Join the waitlist — 500+ already have."
What's on screen: The waitlist card from the landing page. The email "you@company.com" is typed in, a cursor clicks "Join the Waitlist", and the success message "You're on the list!" appears. Avatars and "500+ already joined" show beneath, ending on the NovaMind AI wordmark.
Text on screen: "Get Early Access", "Join the Waitlist", "You're on the list!", "500+ already joined"
Sequential / interaction: Typing, then the click, then success, then the avatars pop in.
Sound effect: Typing.mp3 while typing; Click.mp3 on the button; Notification.mp3 on success.
Transition: Ends on the wordmark hold.
